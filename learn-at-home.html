How It Works

Two courses in one, because learning to drive isn't a solo job

Most people learning outside of paid lessons practise with a parent, partner, or friend who's never been shown how to teach any of this properly. We're building for both of you.

L

Learner Track

Around 30 videos taking you from your first time behind the wheel through to test day — car control, junctions, roundabouts, manoeuvres, and test-ready confidence.

S

Supervisor Track

Around 25 videos for whoever's sitting in the passenger seat — how to coach each skill calmly, what to say (and what not to), and how to actually help rather than just hope for the best.

Please Note

This supports supervised practice — it doesn't replace it

UK law requires learners to practise with a supervisor who's 21+, has held a full licence for at least 3 years, in a properly insured car displaying L-plates. This course trains both of you to make the most of that time — it isn't a way around the requirement.
