Before You Start

Eligibility to become an ADI

Requirements below reflect the current DVSA rules as published on GOV.UK at the time of writing — always confirm the latest position at gov.uk/become-car-driving-instructor before enrolling anyone, as DVSA fees and criteria are reviewed periodically.
✓

Age 21+

You must be at least 21 to be registered as an ADI (you can start training earlier).

✓

Licence history

You'll need to have held a full GB or NI driving licence for a qualifying period — we confirm your exact eligibility at your first meeting.

✓

Background checks

A new enhanced DBS check is required regardless of any previous checks you hold. As a Scotland-based instructor, you'll also need to join the PVG Scheme through Disclosure Scotland — a requirement for all ADIs and trainees since April 2025.

✓

Fit and proper person

DVSA assesses character and conduct as part of your application — we'll talk you through this honestly upfront.

The Pathway

The DVSA qualifying process, step by step

This is the official route to becoming a registered Approved Driving Instructor. We support and coach you through every stage.

Eligibility check, DBS & PVG application

We confirm you meet DVSA's requirements and help you apply for your enhanced DBS check, plus your PVG Scheme membership through Disclosure Scotland — both required before you can register.

ADI Part 1 — Theory & Hazard Perception

A theory and hazard perception test covering road procedure, traffic signs, driving test disabilities and law, and instructional techniques. Current DVSA fee: £81. We run structured revision sessions to prepare you.

ADI Part 2 — Driving Ability

An advanced-standard driving test, assessed to a higher bar than a normal practical test. Current DVSA fee: £111. We provide dedicated Part 2 coaching once your Part 1 is passed.

Optional: Trainee Licence (PDI)

Once Part 2 is passed, you can apply for a trainee licence to legally start teaching under our supervision and earning while you train for Part 3. Current DVSA fee: £140, valid 6 months.

ADI Part 3 — Instructional Ability

Assessed on your ability to actually teach a pupil, not just drive. Current DVSA fee: £111. This is where our in-house mentoring makes the biggest difference.

Registration & your franchise place

Once you pass Part 3, DVSA registration currently costs £300 (renewed every 4 years alongside a standards check). The moment you're on the register, your place in The Driver Academy franchise is confirmed.

You have 2 years from passing Part 1 to complete Parts 2 and 3, or you'll need to start again from Part 1. We build your timetable to comfortably beat that window.
