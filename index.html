The Driver Academy Standard

Coaching built to produce genuinely confident drivers

01

A rigorous, DVSA-aligned method

Every lesson follows the national standard for driving in full, not a shortcut version — so pupils build the skill and confidence to drive well long after test day, not just enough to scrape through it.
